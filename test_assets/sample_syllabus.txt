Class 10 Science - Syllabus
Subject: Science
Class: 10

Unit 1 Chemical Substances - Nature and Behaviour

1.1 Chemical Reactions and Equations
Chemical equations, balanced chemical equations, and the types of reactions such as
combination, decomposition, displacement and double displacement. Oxidation and
reduction, corrosion and rancidity, and their everyday examples.

1.2 Acids, Bases and Salts
Definition of acids and bases, indicators, pH scale and its importance in daily life.
Preparation and uses of common salts, plaster of Paris, bleaching powder and baking soda.

1.3 Metals and Non-metals
Physical and chemical properties of metals and non-metals, reactivity series, ionic
compounds, and the extraction of metals. Corrosion and its prevention.

1.4 Carbon and its Compounds
Covalent bonding in carbon, allotropes, homologous series and functional groups.
Nomenclature, chemical properties of carbon compounds, ethanol and ethanoic acid,
soaps and detergents.

Unit 2 World of Living

2.1 Life Processes
Nutrition, respiration, transportation and excretion in plants and animals. Structure
and function of the human heart, transport of water in plants, and the nephron.

2.2 Control and Coordination
Nervous system, reflex action, human brain, and hormones in animals. Coordination in
plants through plant hormones such as auxin and abscisic acid.

2.3 How do Organisms Reproduce
Asexual and sexual reproduction, reproduction in human beings, reproductive health and
contraception. Significance of variation in species survival.

2.4 Heredity and Evolution
Mendel's experiments, inherited and acquired traits, sex determination, and the basic
ideas of speciation, fossils and human evolution.

Unit 3 Natural Phenomena

3.1 Reflection and Refraction of Light
Laws of reflection and refraction, image formation by spherical mirrors and lenses,
mirror and lens formula, magnification, and refractive index.

3.2 Human Eye and the Colourful World
Structure of the human eye, defects of vision and their correction, dispersion of
light through a prism, atmospheric refraction, scattering and the blue colour of the sky.

3.3 Electricity
Electric current, potential difference, Ohm's law, resistance and resistivity, series
and parallel circuits, heating effect of current, and electric power.

3.4 Magnetic Effects of Electric Current
Magnetic field and field lines, field due to a current-carrying conductor, force on a
conductor, electric motor, electromagnetic induction and the electric generator.

Unit 4 Effects of Current and Natural Resources

4.1 Sources of Energy
Conventional and non-conventional sources of energy, solar cells, wind, tidal, hydro
and geothermal energy, and environmental consequences of energy use.

4.2 Our Environment
Ecosystem, food chains and food webs, trophic levels, flow of energy, and the impact
of human activities. Ozone depletion and waste management.

4.3 Sustainable Management of Natural Resources
Conservation of forests and wildlife, water resources, coal and petroleum, and the
three R's: reduce, reuse and recycle.
